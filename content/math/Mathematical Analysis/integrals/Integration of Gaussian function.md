Integration by substitution

$$
\begin{align}
\int\_{-\infty}^{+\infty} e^{-x^2} dx  =& \sqrt{ \int\_{-\infty}^{+\infty} e^{-s^2} ds \int e^{-t^2} dt } \\
\=& \sqrt{\int\_{-\infty}^{+\infty} e^{-(s^2+t^2)} ds dt} \\
\=& \sqrt{\int\_{0}^{2\pi} \int\_{0}^{+\infty} e^{-r^2} rdr d\theta} \\
\=& \sqrt{\int\_{0}^{2\pi} \int\_{0}^{+\infty} e^{-r^2} rdr d\theta} \\
\=& \sqrt{\pi}
\end{align}
$$
