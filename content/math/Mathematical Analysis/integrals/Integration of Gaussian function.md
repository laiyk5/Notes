
Integration by substitution

$$
\begin{align}
\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} e^{-x^2} dx  =& \sqrt{ \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} e^{-s^2} ds \int e^{-t^2} dt } \\
=& \sqrt{\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} e^{-(s^2+t^2)} ds dt} \\
=& \sqrt{\int_{0}^{2\pi} \int_{0}^{+\infty} e^{-r^2} rdr d\theta} \\
=& \sqrt{\int_{0}^{2\pi} \int_{0}^{+\infty} e^{-r^2} rdr d\theta} \\
=& \sqrt{\pi}
\end{align}
$$
