# Definition

Riemann Sum
$$
\sum\_{i=1}^{n-1} f(t\_{i}) (x\_{i+1} - x\_{i})
$$
Riemann Integral

$\forall \epsilon, \exists \delta >0, \forall x\_{0}, \dots, x\_{n}$ and $\forall t\_{1}, \dots, t\_{n-1}$, as long as $\lambda = \max (x\_{i+1} - x\_{i}) < \delta$, then $| \sum\_{i=1}^{n-1}f(t\_{i}) (x\_{i+1} - x\_{i}) - S| < \epsilon$

Then $S$ is the limit of Riemann sum

# Properties

$$
\int\_{a}^b (\alpha f(x) + \beta g(x) )dx = \alpha \int\_{a}^{b} f(x) dx + \beta \int\_{a}^b g(x) dx
$$

This is due to the definition of Riemann integral and linear properties of limits. [[Limits#Rules with Real Number Operations]]
