# Definition

Riemann Sum
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n-1} f(t_{i}) (x_{i+1} - x_{i})
$$
Riemann Integral

$\forall \epsilon, \exists \delta >0, \forall x_{0}, \dots, x_{n}$ and $\forall t_{1}, \dots, t_{n-1}$, as long as $\lambda = \max (x_{i+1} - x_{i}) < \delta$, then $| \sum_{i=1}^{n-1}f(t_{i}) (x_{i+1} - x_{i}) - S| < \epsilon$

Then $S$ is the limit of Riemann sum
# Properties
$$
\int_{a}^b (\alpha f(x) + \beta g(x) )dx = \alpha \int_{a}^{b} f(x) dx + \beta \int_{a}^b g(x) dx
$$

This is due to the definition of Riemann integral and linear properties of limits. [[math/Mathematical Analysis/Limits#Rules with Real Number Operations]]
